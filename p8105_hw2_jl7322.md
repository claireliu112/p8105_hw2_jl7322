##### Description：

The cleaned NYC transit dataset contains 1868 rows, and 19 columns. I
cleaned variable names and retained the variables requested (line,
station, name, station latitude, station longitude, routes served,
entry, vending, entrance type, and ADA compliance). I convert variable
`entry` from `YES` and `NO` character values to logical values (True and
False). The data are not very tidy because the route information are
stored in `route1` to `route11`. It will be better to store them
separately as route name and route number.

##### What proportion of station entrances / exits without vending allow entrance?
